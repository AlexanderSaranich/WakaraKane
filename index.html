名前の意味

最初に習う数学用語の一つ「和」（足し算の結果）から、大学で出会う「環」（足し算と掛け算ができる代数的構造）へ。どちらも足し算から始まる言葉です。易しい言葉から難しい言葉まで、一本の道としてつながっていることを表しています。

誰のためのサイトか

  • 数学用語を日本語で正確に覚えたい人（日本語を学ぶ人、留学生、日本の大学で数学を学ぶ人を含む）
  • 英語で学んだ数学を日本語で言えるようになりたい人、またはその逆
  • 言葉の意味だけでなく、なぜその概念があるのかまで理解したい人

おすすめの使い方

  1. 「今日」で 1 日の問題数と学習中のレベルを決める。
  2. 上の階段から自分のレベルを開き、「まず押さえたい用語」を読む。
  3. 用語を開いて、定義・なぜこの用語があるのか・語源を読む。
  4. 「問題集で解く」で実際に解き、「練習問題」で言葉として定着させる。
  5. 翌日以降は「今日の復習」から始める。

正確さについて

定義は日本の大学数学で標準的な流儀に従い、流儀が分かれるもの（自然数に 0 を含むか、環に 1 を仮定するか、体が可換か など）は各用語の「注意」に書いています。語源や歴史のうち諸説あるものは「〜とされる」「伝承」と明記しました。用語の標準は文部省『学術用語集 数学編』、岩波『数学辞典』などで確かめることをおすすめします。誤りに気づいたらお知らせください。

About this site

Wa kara Kan e — from “sum” to “ring”

A study site for learning Japanese mathematical terminology, from elementary-school arithmetic (和, wa, “sum”) to graduate-level mathematics (層, sō, “sheaf”). Each term is taught through its definition, reading, English equivalent, etymology and the reason it exists, and practised by solving real problems.

605terms

6levels

35problem units

961written problems

The name

和 (wa, the result of addition) is one of the first math words a Japanese child learns; 環 (kan, a ring — an algebraic structure with addition and multiplication) is one met at university. Both begin with addition. The name expresses that the easy words and the hard words lie on one continuous path.

Who it is for

  • Anyone who wants to learn mathematical terms precisely in Japanese — including learners of Japanese, international students and students of mathematics at Japanese universities.
  • People who learned mathematics in English and want to say it in Japanese, or the other way round.
  • Anyone who wants to understand not just what a term means but why the concept exists.

What you can do

A six-level glossary
Elementary, middle school, high school, early university, upper university and graduate. Every term has a definition, example, reading (furigana), English equivalent, notes on differing conventions and related terms. Open a level from the staircase at the top to read a summary of that stage.
Why the term exists
What problem the concept was created to solve, with a short history of who introduced it and when for many terms.
Etymology
The reading and meaning of each kanji, the original Latin, Greek, German or French word, and how the Japanese translation came about.
Learning resources
Links to the matching pages on 中学校数学・学習サイト (math.005net.com), 数学の景色 (mathlandscape.com) and Math is Fun, each checked to exist.
Flashcard drills
Type-in flashcards: kanji → reading, definition → term, etymology → term, Japanese ↔ English. Grading tolerates variant spellings and notation.
Problem sets (問題集)
Randomly generated problems — from arithmetic to quadratic inequalities, imaginary roots, calculus and determinants — graded by evaluating your answer as a mathematical expression. Proof by contradiction and induction come with checked key steps and model proofs. Every term has problems.
Keeping you going
A daily goal, a day streak, a win streak, spaced review that brings missed terms back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, personalised recommendations, and a mathematician’s quote whenever you miss.

Suggested way to use it

  1. On 今日 (Today), set a daily goal and the level you are studying.
  2. Open your level from the staircase and read the key terms.
  3. Open a term and read its definition, why it exists and its etymology.
  4. Solve it with 「問題集で解く」, then fix the vocabulary with the flashcard drills.
  5. From the next day on, start with 今日の復習 (today’s review).

Accuracy

Definitions follow the conventions standard in Japanese university mathematics; where conventions differ (whether 0 is a natural number, whether rings have a 1, whether 体 must be commutative) the term’s note says so. Uncertain etymologies and historical anecdotes are marked as traditional or hedged. For authoritative usage, consult the Ministry of Education’s 『学術用語集 数学編』 or Iwanami’s 『数学辞典』. Corrections are welcome.

Your data

Your learning record (learned terms, misses, goals, streaks, review schedule) is stored only in your own browser. It is never sent to a server and it is not shared across devices or browsers. You can erase it with 「学習記録を消去」 on the 復習 (Review) tab.

The page uses Google Fonts, so opening it connects to Google’s servers to download the fonts (this sends your IP address and similar request data to Google). There is no analytics or advertising.

Design
