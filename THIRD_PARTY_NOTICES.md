## Quotations / 名言
All quotations shown in the app (`progress.js`) are by authors who died long ago and whose works are in the public domain:
Euclid, Archimedes, Galileo Galilei, René Descartes, Pierre de Fermat, Blaise Pascal, Pierre-Simon Laplace, Carl Friedrich Gauss, Niels Henrik Abel, Leopold Kronecker, Georg Cantor, Sofia Kovalevskaya, Henri Poincaré, David Hilbert.
